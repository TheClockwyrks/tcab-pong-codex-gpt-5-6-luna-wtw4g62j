# Carom

Carom is a fast top-down paddle duel where every rally is a small bank-shot
puzzle. The ball crosses a dark, luminous field between two responsive paddles
while a pair of swaying, rotating bars changes the angles available in play.

In Solo, the right paddle reads the incoming line and returns toward the center
when it has no threat to meet. In Versus, two players share the keyboard. Each
point begins with a short serve countdown, then the pace rises as paddle hits
build speed.

The signature move is spin. Move through the ball at contact to curve its flight
after it leaves the paddle, then let the obstacles turn that curve into a bank
shot. Hold your nerve at match point: the winner must lead by two.
